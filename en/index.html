Brick Cascade Privacy Policy

First effective: October 8, 2026 · Revised: October 15, 2026 (한국어)

This is an English translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean original applies.

Mosaic Kite Studio (the “Operator”) takes the privacy of users of the mobile game Brick Cascade (Android package games.mosaickite.brickcascade) seriously and explains below how information is handled.

1. Information the game stores itself

Game progress, personal best scores, the number of completed games, cumulative scores, tutorial progress, the language, the background music and sound effect settings, the balance of stars (the in-game currency) together with their earning and spending history, upgrade levels and the daily attendance date, and the ad removal purchase state are stored only on the user's device. The Operator does not send this information to a server and does not link it to an account. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when the app data is cleared with the operating system's feature, and deleted stars and game records cannot be restored. The ad removal purchase can be restored from the purchase history of the same Google account.

Stars are earned only by playing and are used only inside the game; they are not currently sold for money. The daily attendance uses the current date of the device, and the game does not separately send this date to the outside.

This game has no account registration, no entry of name, email address or phone number, no access to location, photos or the microphone, no chat, and no server of its own. We have not added a separate analytics or error collection tool; the information handled by the advertising SDK and the billing library is described in sections 2 and 3 below.

2. Advertising service (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

This game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. In the closed test stage, a rewarded test ad provided by Google may be shown only when the user chooses a reward button (for example, continue or an item reward); ads do not appear by themselves. After the official release, real ads may be provided. Because the SDK is included even if no ad is watched, the SDK is initialized when the app starts, and Google may automatically collect or process the following information for ad delivery, analytics and fraud prevention, and share it with Google.

  • IP address and the approximate location inferred from it
  • Product interactions such as launching the app, and app and SDK performance and diagnostic information
  • Device and account identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID, where applicable)

When an ad is shown, the above information, together with ad interactions and video viewing information, may be processed for ad delivery, frequency management, performance measurement and fraud prevention. Before official ads are provided, we will present a privacy choices message in the regions where it is required and let users review or change their choice again.

For users who have bought the ad removal product, banner, interstitial and rewarded ads are neither requested nor shown. The advertising SDK itself, however, remains part of the app.

Users can reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings.

Advertising processor: Google. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy. The information may be processed in the regions where Google operates its services (including outside your country).

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

This game offers one product, “Remove ads” (a one-time purchase), through Google Play Billing. Payment, refunds and purchase restoration are handled by Google Play, and the Operator does not collect or store full payment card numbers. To confirm whether the product has been bought, the game queries Google Play for the purchase history when the app starts or is opened again, and stores the confirmed purchase state (bought or not bought) on the user's device. The Operator does not run a server of its own to confirm purchases. A refund or cancellation is reflected the next time Google Play is queried, so it may take some time to take effect.

The billing feature uses the Unity IAP 5.4 library of Unity Technologies (“Unity”). According to Unity's official guidance, this library processes the player ID, the Unity installation ID, device information, session IDs and the country as information needed for the feature to work, and no setting is provided to turn this off or to decline it. When a purchase is started, completed or confirmed in this game, or when a purchase fails, the billing library may send the following information to Unity's servers.

  • Product ID, product name and description, local price and currency
  • Transaction ID and Google Play purchase receipt information
  • Session identifier and Unity identifiers such as the Unity installation ID (where generated)
  • App version, app installation time and app package name
  • Device information such as the device model, operating system version, language and region settings and storage size
  • Country or region, and the IP address that accompanies the communication (and the approximate location inferred from it)

This game does not provide an email address, a name or any separate user account information to the billing library, and does not link Unity's optional identifiers (such as advertising identifiers or external analytics service identifiers). Unity processes this information to provide the billing feature and for analytics; order information held by Unity is kept until it is deleted, and service logs are kept for 90 days (according to Unity's official guidance). For details, see the Unity Privacy Policy and the Unity IAP privacy overview.

4. Purpose of processing and retention period

  • Local game information (including stars and the ad removal purchase state): restoring game progress, showing personal records and the currency, providing the purchase benefit / while the app data remains
  • Advertising-related information: for the period determined by Google's policies and the user's choices
  • Payment-related information: for the period needed to provide the purchase entitlement, restore it, handle refunds and meet legal obligations
  • Information processed by the billing library (Unity): providing the billing feature and analytics / for the period set by Unity's policies (order information until it is deleted, service logs for 90 days)
  • Inquiries (email): destroyed without delay once the purpose of handling the inquiry is achieved; where the law requires keeping them, they are kept only for the period set by that law. The game app itself does not store inquiries.

5. User rights and choices

Users can reset or delete the advertising ID in the operating system settings, and can remove local game information by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. Privacy-related inquiries and requests to exercise rights can be made to the contact below.

Data deletion requests are received at the support email below. If the Operator holds any information, it will be deleted to the extent possible; for information stored on the device we will explain how to clear the app data or remove the app, and for information managed by Android or Google, such as the advertising ID, we will point to the relevant feature. Information that third parties such as Google manage themselves cannot be deleted by the Operator directly and is handled through that provider's management features.

If you want information processed by Unity through the billing library to be deleted, please tell us at the email below. This app has no feature to delete that information directly; the Operator will forward the request to Unity or explain how to make the request to Unity directly.

6. Children's information

The target users of this game are aged 16 or older. The game is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from such children.

7. Security

This game does not keep user information on a separate server. Third-party SDKs are managed so that versions supporting encrypted transmission are used, and unnecessary SDKs and permissions are reviewed.
